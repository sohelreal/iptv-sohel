Learn More About IPTV Sohel

A media player for Roku devices.

What is IPTV Sohel?

IPTV Sohel is an application designed to let users connect and play media from IPTV services and content sources that they provide themselves. The app is a player interface; it is not an IPTV subscription service or a source of television programming.

How it works

  1. Install IPTV Sohel on a compatible Roku device.
  2. Open the app and follow its on-screen instructions.
  3. Enter connection details for an IPTV service or playlist that you are authorized to use, if supported by your app version.
  4. Browse and play the content made available by that third-party service.

What you need

  • A compatible Roku streaming device or Roku TV.
  • A reliable internet connection.
  • Your own active IPTV service, playlist, or media source, where supported.
  • All necessary rights and permissions to access the content.

Content and subscriptions

IPTV Sohel does not provide or sell channels, movies, series, playlists, or IPTV subscriptions. Users are responsible for selecting lawful services and ensuring they have permission to access the content they play. Availability, channel lists, video quality, and service terms are determined by the third-party provider.

Features and compatibility

Available features depend on the installed app version and the capabilities of the connected content source. Not every provider, playlist format, stream, or media feature is guaranteed to work on every Roku device.

Need assistance?

For help using the application, visit the IPTV Sohel Support page or email sohelreal@gmail.com. For account, subscription, or content questions, contact your IPTV service provider.
