IPTV Sohel support

Help and troubleshooting for the IPTV Sohel Roku application.

App will not open

Restart your Roku device and check that Roku OS is up to date.

Login or connection problem

Check the server address and account details with your IPTV service provider. Make sure the service is active.

Buffering or playback issue

Check your internet connection and try another stream. Stream availability and quality are controlled by the third-party provider and your network.

Missing channels or content

Contact the provider that supplied your playlist or account. IPTV Sohel does not control provider catalogues.

Need to remove saved details

Use the account or settings options available in the app. You can also uninstall the app from your Roku device.

Contact support

Email us about the app. Include your Roku device model, app version, a description of the issue, and the steps that reproduce it.

sohelreal@gmail.com

Do not send your IPTV password, full playlist URL, or other account credentials.

Third-party IPTV services

For subscription, billing, account activation, channel availability, or permission to view content, contact your IPTV service provider directly. Those services are independent of IPTV Sohel.
