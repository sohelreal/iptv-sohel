IPTV Sohel Support

Help and troubleshooting for the IPTV Sohel Roku application.

About IPTV Sohel

IPTV Sohel is a media-player application for Roku devices. Users connect their own third-party IPTV services and are responsible for having permission to access the streams and content they use. IPTV Sohel does not provide IPTV subscriptions, channels, playlists, movies, or television programs.

Common troubleshooting steps

  • App will not open: Restart your Roku device and check that Roku OS is up to date.
  • Login or connection problem: Check the server address and account details with your IPTV service provider. Make sure the service is active.
  • Buffering or playback issue: Check your internet connection and try another stream. Stream availability and quality are controlled by the third-party provider and your network.
  • Missing channels or content: Contact the provider that supplied your playlist or account. IPTV Sohel does not control provider catalogues.
  • Need to remove saved details: Use the account or settings options available in the app. You can also uninstall the app from your Roku device.

Contact support

If you need help with the IPTV Sohel application, email:

sohelreal@gmail.com

When contacting support, include your Roku device model, app version, a description of the issue, and the steps that reproduce it. Do not send your IPTV password, full playlist URL, or other account credentials.

Third-party IPTV services

For subscription, billing, account activation, channel availability, or permission to view content, contact your IPTV service provider directly. Those services are independent of IPTV Sohel.
