Privacy Policy

IPTV Sohel · Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the IPTV Sohel application (“IPTV Sohel,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the application on a Roku device.

1. What IPTV Sohel does

IPTV Sohel is a media-player application. It does not itself provide, sell, host, or license television channels, movies, series, playlists, or IPTV subscriptions. Users choose and connect their own third-party IPTV services and are responsible for ensuring that they are authorized to access the content provided by those services.

2. Information you enter

If you choose to connect an IPTV service, you may enter information such as a server address, username, password, playlist URL, or other connection details. The application uses the information you provide to connect to the service you selected and request playback or related information.

Connection details may be retained on your Roku device if the app provides a remember-login or saved-account feature. Anyone with access to your device or its app data may potentially be able to access information saved there. Remove saved accounts and sign out before allowing another person to use your device, where those options are available.

3. Information collected by the app developer

IPTV Sohel is not intended to require your real name, postal address, or payment-card information. We do not operate the IPTV service that you connect to. The third-party service provider may receive your connection details, IP address, device/network information, and viewing or usage requests when your device connects to that provider. That provider's own privacy policy governs its handling of such information.

When you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, or a description of a problem. Please do not email passwords, complete IPTV credentials, or other sensitive information.

Technical disclosure: The information actually stored, transmitted, or collected can depend on the app version, enabled features, Roku platform services, and any third-party software included in the app. This policy must be kept accurate if those features change.

4. How information is used

Information you enter is used to provide the player features you request, connect to your selected service, support playback, remember settings where available, troubleshoot problems you report, and maintain or improve the application.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information you enter to connect to an IPTV provider is sent to the provider or relevant service endpoint as needed for the connection and playback features you use. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the application.

6. Data retention and security

Saved connection details and settings may remain on your Roku device until removed, overwritten, or deleted with the app or device data. Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain support records. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

IPTV Sohel is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app and any third-party IPTV services connected through it.

8. Third-party services

The app may connect to IPTV servers or services selected by the user. We do not control those services, their content, availability, security, or privacy practices. Review the terms and privacy policy of each service you use.

9. Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time, remove saved connection details where the app provides that option, and uninstall the app from your Roku device. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests. We may need to verify a request before responding.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
